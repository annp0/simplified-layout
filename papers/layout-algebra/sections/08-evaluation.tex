\section{Evaluation}\label{sec:eval}

The implementation\footnote{\url{https://github.com/annp0/simplified-layout}}
is \evalLibLines{} lines of OCaml, over \evalLibMliLines{} lines of
interface; the tests are \evalTestLines{}. Every figure quoted below is
generated from a run of the test suite (\texttt{papers/eval.sh}), not
transcribed. The random generators are seeded, so a rerun with the toolchain
the repository's README names reproduces them.

\paragraph{The decision procedure is correct.} The criterion of
Lemma~\ref{lem:dense} agrees with enumeration on \evalDenseRandom{} random
layouts. The scan of
Theorem~\ref{thm:decide} is compared with an oracle that tries every chain of
divisors of $n$ and checks the strides it forces at every point. Over every
function $g : [0,n) \to [0,k)$ with $g(0) = 0$, for
$n \in \{2,3,4,6,8,9,12\}$ and $k = 5$ if $n \le 4$, $3$ otherwise, that is
\evalAxisFunctions{} functions, the two accept the same \evalAxisStrided{},
with the same chain.

\paragraph{CuTe's conditions are not checked, and the failure is silent.}
The sweep quoted in Section~\ref{sec:intro} takes every rank-2 layout
$(N_0,N_1){:}(d_0,d_1)$ with $N_0, N_1 \in \{2,3,4\}$, $d_0 \in \{1,2\}$ and
$d_1 \le 16$, against cotargets $M \in \{12,24,48\}$, keeping those for which
the complement formula is defined at all; CuTe rejects $d_1 < N_0 d_0$ with
a \texttt{static\_assert}, the one condition it does enforce. Every entry is a
static integer, so nothing here is hidden from the compiler. Each complement is
checked against the postconditions CuTe's own unit test asserts: that
$\cosize(A, A^{*})$ reach $M$, that $A^{*}$ be ordered, and that $A^{*}$ and
$A$ be disjoint. Cases are classified by \emph{both} of the complement's
divisibility conditions, $N_0 d_0 \mid d_1$ and $N_1 d_1 \mid M$. Of the
\cuteCompTotal{} cases, the \cuteCompSatisfied{} satisfying both all pass; of
the \cuteCompViolated{} that violate one or the other,
\cuteCompViolatedBroken{} fail. The conditions are therefore sufficient and
not necessary, which is exactly why their absence bites: most violations
happen to be harmless, nothing distinguishes those from the rest, and the ones
that are not harmless fail the codomain bound, never orderedness or
disjointness, without a diagnostic. The mechanism is plain in the formula:
its shape entries are quotients, so when a divisibility fails the integer
division truncates and the complement covers less than it should. For
$(2,2){:}(1,3)$ at $M = 24$ the entry $d_1 / N_0 d_0 = 3/2$ becomes $1$, and
$\cosize$ comes out $23$. Measured against CUTLASS 4.8; the
harness is \texttt{study/run.sh} and needs no GPU.

\paragraph{It matters in practice.} The tests build \evalLayouts{} layouts,
from CuTe's documented examples, CUTLASS's atoms, the usage table of
Section~\ref{sec:idioms} and the laws of Section~\ref{sec:ops}, and check
each against its emitted expression at every coordinate. Of those,
\evalLayoutsAffine{} are affine in their coordinates, \evalLayoutsRefined{}
are layouts of a refinement with one coordinate split, and
\evalLayoutsPipeline{} are not layouts and are emitted as composites. On
\evalPairs{} random composites of a dense bijection with a padded storage
map, \evalPairsStrided{} are layouts of some refinement, and their emitted
forms contain \evalPairsOpsEmitted{} divisions and moduli where the
composites contain \evalPairsOpsPipeline{}. With a random swizzle on the
storage map, none of \evalSwizzled{} is a layout.

\paragraph{The algebra is enough for a kernel.} The SM90 GMMA $64 \times
128$ accumulator, built from the SM80 \texttt{mma.m16n8k16} $C$ fragment as
\[
\divideop\big(\mathrm{canonical}((64,128)),\ (16,8)\big) \circ
\interleave\big(F_C,\ \mathrm{canonical}((4,16))\big),
\]
equals NVIDIA's printed layout at all \evalSmNinetyCoords{} coordinates. A
GEMM mainloop
built from the operations alone, an $8 \times 32$ tile of 16-bit elements
copied in 16-byte vectors by one warp into two shared-memory stages of the
SW64 atom and read back by \texttt{ldmatrix}, with the writer and reader of
Section~\ref{sec:cute} and the stages by $\repeatop$, is consistent: for
every element, the address the copy writes is the address the \texttt{mma}
operand is read from.
